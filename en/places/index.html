A Brasileira

A Brasileira at Rua Garrett 120–122 in Chiado opened in 1905, founded by Adriano Telles to sell Brazilian coffee. Its Art Nouveau façade and mirrored, wood-panelled interior, designed by Manuel Norte Júnior, turned it into a Lisbon literary salon; the bronze Fernando Pessoa seated outside was added for the poet’s centenary in 1988.

A Ginjinha

A Ginjinha at Largo de São Domingos 8 is Lisbon’s standing-room shrine to ginjinha, the sour-cherry liqueur. The brand traces its roots to an 1840 tavern at Rua das Portas de Santo Antão 27; the present shop was refurbished in 1910 by Francisco Espiñeira Couziño. A small glass, an optional cherry, and a few minutes at the counter are the whole ritual.
